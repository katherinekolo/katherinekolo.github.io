A little about me.

Hi! My name is Katherine Kolodziejczak, and I am versatile writer, editor, and communications specialist based in the Greater Boston area. I've always loved reading, writing, design, and technology, and I welcome the opportunity to utizlize these skills in a professional setting. I'd love to help you or your company in any way I can.

I consider myself a hardworking, reliable, and meticulous individual with strong writing, communication, and technical skills, with the ability to quickly learn new software and technologies. My versatile writing skills range from long-form content, such as research papers and grant proposals, to marketing copy, to technical writing such as software manuals. I also have experience with web design and development, which included creating this website using HTML and CSS.

I believe that user experience is the most important aspect of any type of written content, from the shortest marketing copy to the longest technical manual; I strive to always create content that is catered to the intended audience in a clear and engaging way.
